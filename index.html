Multi-Beam Acoustic Structured Illumination: Implementation and Evaluation

Implemented and evaluated multi-beam Acoustic Structured Illumination patterns using established Gerchberg–Saxton and STAR phase-retrieval algorithms. Evaluated beamforming and imaging performance through simulation and hardware experiments.

Implemented Golay, Barker, and Hadamard coded-excitation approaches and integrated multi-angle plane-wave and super-resolution ultrasound workflows.

Developed a hydrophone-based laboratory setup for measuring and characterizing ultrasound pressure fields and validating acoustic beam profiles.
